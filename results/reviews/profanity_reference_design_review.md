# New profanity artifacts with the published PV procedure

Claude reviewed the strategy, exact trait/question descriptions, semantic gates
and native-wrapper choice before implementation. The coordinator accepts the
corrected design in docs/profanity_reference_protocol.md. Code acceptance and a
committed rendered packet/source manifest still precede any artifact invocation;
generated-artifact semantic acceptance separately precedes every Qwen response.

The reference is the archived Apache-2.0 Persona Vectors general generation
template, with only its three slots filled. These are new data from the pinned
native Claude, not author profanity data or Claude3.7 outputs. Every positive
instruction must explicitly request casual non-slur swearing; its clean negative
must retain the same casual register, warmth and helpfulness. This avoids
confounding swearing with politeness, rudeness or informality. The rubric must
score friendly swearing high and rude clean language low.

Claude requested and the coordinator adopted these corrections:

- Do not send old overlap questions to the generator or append material to the
  author template. Check exact normalized collisions locally after generation.
- Freeze slots, rendered packet, overlap allowlist and eleven-word lexicon before
  invocation; split the forty generated questions with seed 703, then preserve
  the PV evaluation seed 701. Instructions/rubric match across the two splits.
- Check the original evaluation template outside only its trait/description
  regions, with exactly one question and answer placeholder. Root and Claude
  review the full accepted artifact before promotion; a semantic failure or
  refusal closes artifact_null without reroll or hand edits.
- Reuse the unchanged audited tools-off evaluator wrapper and medium effort,
  with an explicit frozen task_type sentence identifying dataset generation.
  The exact rendered task and author's return schema are the only other packet
  fields. This is a disclosed role-wrapper adaptation; the system permits JSON
  task instructions and does not prohibit generation.
- Maximum two identical-payload starts. Only structural failures permit a
  second; coordinator inspection precedes an explicit retry so a semantic
  refusal cannot accidentally trigger one. Infrastructure, identity, hash or
  uncertain-start failures remain permanent stops.
- Bind generator source/template, slots/packet, binary/model/version/audit,
  starts/raw responses/receipts, parsed JSON, split and semantic review to the
  active profile. Preserve frozen PV code, with a separate read-only lexical
  supplement bound before elicitation. Lexicon findings are descriptive and
  add no judging calls or selection gates.

The generator and judge share a model and wrapper, unlike the authors' separate
Claude/GPT setup. This risks circularity; the fixed lexical diagnostic supplies
independent but incomplete evidence. Original-scale and calibrated custom scores
remain distinct. Off-target rudeness/sarcasm are reported, not hidden or gated.
The maximum PV budget remains 502 generations and 128 grading starts, plus at
most two separately recorded artifact starts. Combined control remains deferred.

Claude also accepted the exact provenance boundary: a committed aggregate origin
record hashes private first-valid generation records and promoted data; promotion
reconstructs raw response through parse and seed-703 split to identical bytes.
The semantic review names those data hashes. Private records remain retained
and are reverified at promotion, supplement and independent postrun audit. Live
PV commands protect the frozen consumed data directly; no new wrapper or nested
historical rehash is added. Private attempts are not committed merely to satisfy
the frozen helper's committed-provenance guard.

The matched assistant identities are fixed before outputs: "helpful and profane"
versus "helpful and nonprofane". Claude chose this over either an unbalanced
profane/helpful pair or identical identities: it preserves the author's opposing
identity pattern while holding helpfulness equal. Full systems, including the
generated instructions, undergo semantic review for matched warmth/register.

Before any generator call, the coordinator selected Claude's optional template
formatting refinement: normalize whitespace runs for matching and ignore only
the trait token's case; preserve all other content, placeholder names and raw
generated bytes. This avoids treating a blank-line change as a scientific null.
The semantic review also records the complete rendered-system list or its hash,
alongside the exact dataset hashes. No scoring or selection criterion changes.
